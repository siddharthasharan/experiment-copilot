Documentation

How it works

Pick an industry in chat, pick a scenario, confirm or modify the design, and run it. The server generates seeded synthetic data, runs the statistical engine in real stages (data generation, validation, assignment, balance, metrics, guardrails, scorecard), and opens the scorecard beside the chat. Follow-ups like “Exclude the five largest markets” or “Show results for new customers” recompute from the stored experiment data — they are not canned answers.

Decision rules

SRM failure → Invalid. Guardrail failure → Do Not Launch. Significant positive primary with guardrails passed → Launch. Significant negative → Do Not Launch. Otherwise Inconclusive. For DoorDash, a negative contribution as designed also yields Do Not Launch, with the reason stated on the scorecard.

What is precomputed, honestly

The 14 example scenarios are precomputed demonstrations (1M users, seed 42, recorded engine output). They are labelled as such everywhere. The three flagships are generated and analysed live on every run.

Adding an LLM later

The chat orchestrator accepts one optional LLMAdapter.explain() method. It may add an explanation after a deterministic answer. It may not choose scenarios, set configs, compute metrics, or replace a deterministic block. The engine does not change.

Limitations

All data is synthetic. Geo experiments are analysed at geo level, never by counting impressions inside a geo. A geo allocation experiment cannot identify individual publisher causal effects. Segment and follow-up checks are exploratory unless pre-specified.
